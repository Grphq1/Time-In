# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 11, 2026**

Time In ("Time In", "we", "us", "our") is a personal productivity and focus application created and maintained by **Alireza Momeni**.

By using Time In, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

**Account:** Email, name, profile picture, account creation/update timestamps, email verification status. Received from Google or GitHub during sign-in.

**Authentication:** Time In uses **Google and GitHub OAuth only** — no email/password authentication exists. We store only the provider and account identifier to link your account.

**Focus & Session Data:** Session names, focus method, timer durations (focus/break/long-break), timer state, completed focus periods count, session creation/update times, focus history, focus period durations and completion times. Used solely for core functionality and your productivity history.

**Collaborative Sessions:** Sessions you create/join, join timestamps, technical events for session synchronization. Not for arbitrary messages or user-generated content.

**Email Verification:** Temporary verification token, expiration, and consumption status — used only for verification.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- Create and manage your account
- Authenticate you securely
- Verify your email
- Provide focus/timer functionality
- Save and display your focus history
- Create and synchronize collaborative sessions
- Maintain security and reliability
- Respond to support, privacy, or account requests

**No targeted advertising. No analytics. No tracking.**

## 3. Cookies & Sessions

Essential cookies only: session cookie to keep you signed in and protect your account. No advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

## 4. Third-Party Authentication

Google and GitHub sign-in available. Their privacy policies apply to their processing. We only receive name, email, and profile picture.

## 5. Sharing & Security

**No selling your data.** We share only with infrastructure providers (hosting, auth, email) as necessary to operate. Minimal data necessary per purpose.

**Security:** No passwords stored. Sessions secured, access limited to operational necessity. No absolute guarantees — no method is completely secure.

## 6. Retention & Deletion

We retain data while your account exists. Delete your account → we delete associated data, except what we're legally required or permitted to retain (security, fraud prevention, disputes).

To request deletion, email **grphq1@gmail.com** from your account email for verification.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request: access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portable copy of your data.

Email **grphq1@gmail.com** — we'll verify identity and comply.
